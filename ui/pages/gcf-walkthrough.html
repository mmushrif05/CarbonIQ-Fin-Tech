The eight steps — one candidate, from the door to the Fund

Where the entity stands and what is blocking it first; then one candidate comes in on the intake form already filled, is read against the ten-stage cycle, screened and structured, assessed and signed off by a named assessor, packaged for a Concept Note, and is in the GCF disclosure the pipeline yields. Open takes you to the screen with the step already applied and the control to press marked; Start the walkthrough keeps a strip on every screen with the step, what to do, and what to say.

    Language

    • Accreditation is a gate, not a score — Board decision B.36/10. An excluded candidate is one the entity cannot carry as the accredited entity.
    • The assessment is the bank’s own appraisal, signed by a named assessor. Never a decision of the Fund, and never a GCF endorsement.
    • Every figure carries its evidence tier — measured, modelled, benchmark or declared. These are GCF appraisal classes, never PCAF’s 1–5 data-quality scale.
    • A pipeline of financed projects is not the entity’s inventory. SLFRS S2 §29(a) is absent with where the figure comes from; avoided and reduced emissions are stated apart and netted against nothing.
    • The shipped illustrative set and the starter projects say so on their face. Recorded candidates replace them entirely and are never merged with them.
    • State what the figure is, cite the standard that governs it, and stop.
