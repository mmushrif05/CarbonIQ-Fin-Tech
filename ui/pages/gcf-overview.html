What stands behind this figure

The accreditation every gate reads, the evidence tier behind every result, the clause behind every line of the disclosure, and the rules this pipeline is held to — each proved by its own test.
Candidate in focus

Where each candidate sits

The Fund’s ten-stage project activity cycle. One bar per stage the pipeline occupies, the candidates on it, and the GCF ask they carry.

The gate

Accreditation is a gate, not a score — Board decision B.36/10. An excluded candidate is one the entity cannot carry as the accredited entity; a flagged one is eligible with something to verify.

What is blocking, and who holds the key

Every open item the engines raised — what the stage needs, what the six criteria lack, whether the assessment is signed, what the assessor returned — grouped by who closes it. A gap does not stop a stage move; it travels with the candidate.

By candidate, furthest along first

The items that block the next step, each with the clause that asks for it and who answers. A row opens the candidate.

Results on three boundaries, drawn apart

Lifetime mitigation per candidate against its counterfactual — GCF Mitigation Core Indicator 1. An adaptation project’s carbon is a co-benefit on its own line and never in the headline; embodied carbon is a separate boundary and never deducted; financed emissions live on the capital book on PCAF Part A attribution.

Beneficiaries

IRMF Core Indicator 2. Direct and indirect are two indicators and are never summed; every figure carries its evidence tier on the record.

Coming up

Targets the bank has set and the dates the Fund’s service standards imply. A projected date is marked as one and names the standard it rests on.

What the file answers

SLFRS S2 §29(d) opportunities, §29(e) capital deployment, and emissions avoided or reduced stated apart. A pipeline is not the entity’s inventory: §29(a) is absent by rule, with where the figure comes from.

By candidate

One row per candidate, as recorded. The weakest evidence tier is the one a reviewer should ask about first. Each row opens the candidate; the documents beside it are reads anyone may take.

Before submission

What the disclosure still asks the reporting entity for, with the clause. Nothing is written on the entity’s behalf.
