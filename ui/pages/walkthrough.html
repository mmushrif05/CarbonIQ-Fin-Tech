The seven steps — one loan, from the door to the file

The position and the SLFRS S2 file first; then one loan comes in with every field already filled, a second that does not know its emissions is priced on the held sector factor, the engine’s answer is read, it is reviewed and approved, and it is on the dashboard and in the file. Open takes you to the screen with the step already applied and the control to press marked; Start the walkthrough keeps a strip on every screen with the step, what to do, and what to say.

    Language

    • Always PCAF-conformant. Never certified, approved or endorsed: every artefact refuses those words.
    • The shipped factor tables and several baselines are provisional, and say so on screen and in the document. That is a disclosure, not a defect.
    • Assurance mode is self-declared until every governed value resolves to a released baseline and the entity has recorded who assured the figures.
    • SLFRS S2 is IFRS S2 as adopted in Sri Lanka. The document names every paragraph it answers and every one the bank has not yet stated.
    • An SLFRS S2 statement still equal to the illustrative content is marked as such wherever it is printed, on screen and in the document.
    • State what the figure is, cite the standard that governs it, and stop.
