A construction project is not a company

Insurance-associated emissions are normally derived by matching the insured to a company database and reading off a revenue-based proxy. A project under a construction all-risks policy has no company record to match, so that route returns nothing. PCAF Part C §5.3 asks instead for the project's own emissions — which means building them from the bill of quantities.

Company matching

Resolve the insured to a company identifier, take reported or modelled emissions, apply the attribution factor.
