SECTION 2 [coverage] Scope and coverage
    callout [What this report covers]: This report covers one PCAF asset class — sovereign debt (Part A §5.9). It is one input to a Chapter 6 financed-emissions disclosure, not the disclosure: the reporting entity’s own gross scope 1, 2 and 3 inventory is a separate and larger claim, made where the entity states it.
    body: Asset class: Sovereign debt (§5.9). Reporting year: 2024. The position is taken at the fiscal year-end, which is how Part A accounts for financed emissions (Chapter 4).
    body: This report describes one sovereign holding. A hundred per cent of a one-holding inventory is not a claim about the reporting entity’s book, and coverage of the book is stated only in the annual disclosure.

SECTION 3 [gases] Gases and units
    body: Emissions are reported in tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent (tCO2e), using the IPCC 100-year global warming potentials of the latest assessment report. A sovereign figure is the country’s own national inventory reported to the UNFCCC, covering the seven Kyoto Protocol gases the inventory reports.
    table [3 cols] head: Gas | Formula | Where it arises in a national inventory
      row: Carbon dioxide | CO2 | Fuel combustion, industry and land use across the national territory.
      row: Methane | CH4 | Agriculture, waste and fossil-fuel supply in the national inventory.
      row: Nitrous oxide | N2O | Agricultural soils and combustion in the national inventory.
      row: Hydrofluorocarbons | HFCs | Refrigeration and air-conditioning reported to the UNFCCC.
      row: Perfluorocarbons | PFCs | Aluminium and electronics production in the national inventory.

    body: Land-use, land-use change and forestry (LULUCF) is reported as a boundary on scope 1 — both including and excluding it — rather than netted against the fossil inventory (§5.9, p.141).

SECTION 5 [methodology] Methodology
    body: Financed emissions of sovereign debt are the country’s territorial emissions multiplied by an attribution factor: the exposure in international USD over the sovereign’s PPP-adjusted GDP in international USD (§5.9, p.144). The denominator is PPP-adjusted GDP, never equity plus debt: debt alone is a poor denominator — a near-debt-free sovereign would attribute a thousandfold more per dollar — so output stands in for enterprise value (Annex 10.3, pp.201–204).
    body: The engine performs every arithmetic operation. Nothing in this report is computed by a language model, and no figure here rests on one. Each figure traces to the holding the register holds and to the sovereign dataset named below.
    body: Sovereign dataset: sovereign-dataset v1.0.0 (provisional); SHA-256 over the canonical form, <checksum>. Provisional pending a released set: sovereign-dataset. A provisional row carries the gap it stands in for.
    body: The public sources PCAF names for this class (Table 10.3-4, pp.205–206): the UNFCCC data interface, Climate Watch, EDGAR (a four-year lag), OECD trade-embodied CO2 for scope 2 and 3, and World Bank PPP GDP.

SECTION 6 [dataquality] Data quality
    body: PCAF scores sovereign data quality on a 1–5 scale by the source the figure rests on (Table 5.9-6, p.147): verified UNFCCC-reported is 1, unverified is 2, physical energy activity is 3, sectoral revenue is 4, a proxy country is 5. 1 is the highest quality, 5 the lowest; a score is a category, not a mark out of five.
