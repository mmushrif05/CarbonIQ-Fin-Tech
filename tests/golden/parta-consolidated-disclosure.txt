    body: This disclosure is prepared in accordance with SLFRS S2 Climate-related Disclosures, which is IFRS S2 as adopted in Sri Lanka. ANNEX B [annexBasis] Financed emissions — basis of preparation
    body: The basis on which the financed-emissions figures in Annex A were prepared, as PCAF Part A Chapter 6 asks it to be stated: the gases and units, the attribution and estimation method of each asset class, the data-quality score per class, the recalculation protocol, and the uncertainty the figures carry.
    h2: Gases and units
    body: Emissions are reported in tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent (tCO2e), on the 100-year global warming potentials of IPCC AR6, 100-year, as stated by the reporting entity. Each class covers the seven Kyoto Protocol gases as its own section describes: a borrower’s inventory where reported, a national inventory for a sovereign, and whichever gases an emission factor covers where a figure is estimated.
    table: Gas | Formula | Where it arises
      row: Carbon dioxide | CO2 | The dominant gas in every borrower inventory: fuel combustion, process emissions and purchased electricity.
      row: Methane | CH4 | Fossil-fuel supply chains, agriculture and waste in the borrowers financed.
      row: Nitrous oxide | N2O | Combustion and, for agricultural borrowers, fertiliser use.
      row: Hydrofluorocarbons | HFCs | Refrigeration and cooling in a borrower’s operations.
      row: Perfluorocarbons | PFCs | Aluminium smelting and some electronics manufacture.
      row: Sulphur hexafluoride | SF6 | Electrical switchgear at industrial and utility borrowers.
      row: Nitrogen trifluoride | NF3 | Electronics manufacture; accounted for where a borrower reports it.
    body: Biogenic carbon, where reported, is stated separately from the fossil inventory and never netted against it; land-use removals sit within a sovereign’s scope 1 including LULUCF and are never netted against its excluding figure.
    h2: Methodology
    body: Each asset class is attributed on the rule its section of Part A sets. Business loans and unlisted equity: the outstanding amount over the borrower’s total equity plus debt (EVIC where listed), times the borrower’s emissions, reported or estimated by option (§5.2, p.57). Commercial real estate: the outstanding amount over the property value at origination, times the building’s operational scope 1 and 2 from metered energy or from statistics by floor area, the option set by how the energy is known (§5.4, p.79). Sovereign debt: the exposure in international USD over the sovereign’s PPP-adjusted GDP, times its territorial emissions on both LULUCF boundaries (§5.9, p.144). The per-class documents carry each method in full; this disclosure lays their results side by side and computes nothing.
    body: The engine performs every arithmetic operation. Nothing in this disclosure is computed by a language model, and no figure here rests on one.
    body: Regional sector factor set: sector-factors v1.0.0 (provisional), sectors v1.0.0 (vocabulary); SHA-256 over the canonical form, <sha256>. Provisional: sector-factors.
    body: Real-estate energy statistics: real-estate-energy-statistics v1.0.0 (provisional); SHA-256 over the canonical form, <sha256>. Provisional: real-estate-energy-statistics.
    body: Sovereign dataset: sovereign-dataset v1.0.0 (provisional); SHA-256 over the canonical form, <sha256>. Provisional: sovereign-dataset.
    body: Sector intensity bands (the plausibility check): Sector carbon-intensity plausibility bands — illustrative dataset, not a released baseline. These are illustrative until a baseline is released.

    body: A recalculation of the base year is a "shall" under Chapter 6 when the book, the method or a factor changes materially.     h2: What triggers a recalculation
    bullets:
      - Structural change to the book — an acquisition, disposal or merger that moves exposures into or out of the inventory.
      - A change of calculation methodology, or of the emission factors relied on, that materially changes reported emissions.
      - A change in the boundary of what is measured — an asset class or a business line entering or leaving the inventory.
      - Discovery of a material error, or of several errors that are material in aggregate.
      - A released sector-factor set or a released sector intensity band that moves a reported figure by at least the significance threshold.
