SECTION 5 [methodology] Methodology
    h2: Attribution
    callout [Equation]: insurance-associated emissions = attribution factor x project emissions,  where attribution factor = premium / project cost
    body: Attribution follows the gross premium basis. Each policy is attributed against its own project cost and only the results are summed. Attribution is applied per policy against its own project cost; only the results are summed. Premiums and emissions are never pooled before attribution.
    h2: The policy gate
    body: Construction-only cover (CAR/EAR) carries no use stage: B1, B4 and B7 are zero by scope rule, not by omission. Cover extending into occupation (IDI/Property) runs the use stage over the cover period.

SECTION 7 [recalculation] Recalculation and significance threshold
    table [2 cols] head: Item | Stated

      row: Restatement threshold — makes a new version of a locked assessment a restatement | 5% movement in the construction figure
    h2: What triggers a recalculation of base-year emissions
    bullet: Structural change to the book — an acquisition, disposal or merger that moves policies into or out of the inventory.
    bullet: A change of calculation methodology, or of the emission factors relied on, that materially changes reported emissions.
    bullet: A change in the boundary of what is measured — a line of business, module or policy type entering or leaving the inventory.
    bullet: Discovery of a material error, or of several errors that are material in aggregate.
    bullet: A bill of quantities revision that moves a locked assessment by at least the restatement threshold.
    caption: Triggers follow the GHG Protocol Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard, phrased for an insurance book.
    callout [Open item]: No base year is stated for this reporting entity. The report says so rather than implying the current year, because a base year is a claim about history and belongs to the entity, not to its software.
    caption: No figure disclosed for FY2025 has been restated.
